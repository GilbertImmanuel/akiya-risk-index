# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, policy analysts, and local-government staff who need to compare Japanese municipalities on housing vacancy, demand pressure, fiscal capacity, and rail access. They are usually at a desk, reading a map and checking specific places. They know the policy context but not necessarily the statistics. The map is a working reference, not a presentation.

## Product Purpose

The Empty House Atlas shows where structural housing vacancy is concentrated in Japan, which places face weaker demand, and which have the fiscal and transport capacity to respond. The map covers the 1,059 municipal units in the 2023 Housing and Land Survey. It is a starting point for research and policy triage, not a property-level prediction or investment guide.

Success means a reader can switch indicators, read the legend without a methods page, find a specific municipality, and understand the coverage gap without being misled.

## Brand Personality

Factual, calm, cartographic. The voice is plain and specific. It states what the data measure and what they do not. It does not sell, dramatize, or claim more certainty than the survey supports.

## Anti-references

- SaaS dashboards with identical rounded cards, soft shadows, and gradient accents.
- Warm cream backgrounds with terracotta accents and a large display serif used as a marketing hero.
- Big-number hero blocks with three supporting statistics.
- Tiny all-caps labels above every section.
- Interchangeable “insight” copy that could describe any dataset.
- Choropleth maps that color missing places as zero.
- Tiny legends that require a methods page to decode.

## Design Principles

1. The map is the argument. The interface around it stays quiet.
2. Show the measured universe honestly. Missing places are missing, not zero.
3. Use absolute policy thresholds, not relative ranks, so the legend means the same thing in every municipality.
4. Use real names, real values, and complete definitions. No decorative labels.
5. Make it feel like a statistical atlas: ruled columns, maps, footnotes, and readable numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. The page must work by keyboard, show visible focus, keep body text at 4.5:1 contrast or better, and provide text values for every map color. Color palettes must remain readable for common color-vision deficiencies. Respect reduced motion. The mobile layout must remain usable for reading a specific municipality, not only for viewing the national map.
