# Glossary

**Last updated:** 2026-11-12
**Purpose:** Plain-language definitions for a reader with an IT background but no housing or real-estate experience. Japanese terms are included because the primary sources use them.

---

## A. Housing and vacancy statistics

| Term | Japanese | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Dwelling** | 住宅 | A structurally independent unit with at least one living room, a dedicated kitchen sink, a dedicated toilet, and a dedicated entrance. |
| **Vacant house** | 空き家 | A dwelling with no usual resident, excluding temporary occupants and units under construction. |
| **Rental vacancy** | 賃貸用の空き家 | Vacant and offered for rent, new or used. |
| **For-sale vacancy** | 売却用の空き家 | Vacant and offered for sale, new or used. |
| **Secondary residence** | 二次的住宅 | A villa or occasional-use home; not the household's usual residence. |
| **"Other vacant"** | 賃貸・売却用及び二次的住宅を除く空き家 (formerly その他の住宅) | The residual vacancy category: long-term absence, demolition-planned homes, storage, unknown use, and genuine abandonment. **Not** a synonym for "abandoned." |
| **Dilapidation** | 腐朽・破損 | Physical decay or damage recorded by the survey's building inspector. Used to isolate the riskiest vacant houses. |
| **Ruined house** | 廃屋 | A structure too deteriorated to support ordinary family life; **not counted as a dwelling**, so it is outside the vacancy statistics. |
| **Temporary occupants only** | 一時現在者のみ | A dwelling used only during the day or by people sleeping in shifts; not counted as vacant. |
| **Under construction** | 建築中の住宅 | A dwelling not yet complete; not counted as vacant. |
| **Housing and Land Survey** | 住宅・土地統計調査 | The 5-yearly national survey that produces the vacancy statistics. A sample survey, not a census. |
| **Census** | 国勢調査 | The 5-yearly population and household census. |
| **Densely inhabited district** | 人口集中地区 (DID) | A contiguous built-up area with high population density; a proxy for urban character. |
| **Household** | 世帯 | A group of people living together and sharing living expenses; a single person living alone is also a household. |
| **Ordinary household** | 一般世帯 | A household other than an institutional or communal establishment. |

## B. Policy and law

| Term | Japanese | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Vacant Houses Act** | 空家等対策の推進に関する特別措置法 | The 2014/2015 national law on vacant houses. |
| **Specified vacant house** | 特定空家等 | A vacant house that is a serious danger to safety, sanitation, landscape, or the living environment. The severe category. |
| **Poorly managed vacant house** | 管理不全空家等 | A vacant house at risk of becoming a `特定空家等` if left unmanaged. The 2023 amendment created this pre-severe category. |
| **Guidance** | 指導 | A municipal instruction to the owner to improve management. |
| **Recommendation** | 勧告 | A formal municipal recommendation after guidance fails; the key trigger for removing the residential-land tax exception. |
| **Order** | 命令 | A stronger enforcement step for `特定空家等`. |
| **Administrative execution** | 行政代執行 | The municipality demolishes or secures the property and charges the owner. |
| **Simplified execution** | 略式代執行 | A faster execution route where the owner cannot be identified. |
| **Vacant-house plan** | 空家等対策計画 | A municipal plan required/encouraged by the Act. |
| **Support corporation** | 空家等管理活用支援法人 | A designated organisation that can manage, use, or dispose of vacant houses. |
| **Promotion zone** | 空家等活用促進区域 | A zone where redevelopment and use promotion are easier. |
| **Inheritance registration** | 相続登記 | Registering inherited property in the heir's name; mandatory since April 2024. |
| **Address-change registration** | 住所等変更登記 | Registering an owner's address/name change; mandatory from April 2026, with a transition to March 2028. |
| **Vacancy tax proposal** | 空室税 | A DPFP proposal for a national tax on non-residential/investment housing in urban areas. **Not law.** |
| **Kyoto non-residential housing tax** | 非居住住宅利活用促進税 | A Kyoto local tax on non-residential housing, planned for FY2030. |

## C. Taxes and finance

| Term | Japanese | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed-asset tax** | 固定資産税 | An annual local tax on land, buildings, and depreciable assets. |
| **Urban-planning tax** | 都市計画税 | An additional local tax in urbanisation areas. |
| **Residential-land exception** | 住宅用地特例 | A reduction of the fixed-asset tax base for residential land: 1/6 for ≤200 m², 1/3 for >200 m². |
| **Tax base** | 課税標準 | The value on which the tax rate is applied; not the same as the final tax bill. |
| **Assessment date** | 賦課期日 | The date used to determine the tax liability; for fixed-asset tax, 1 January. |
| **Fiscal strength index** | 財政力指数 | A municipal fiscal-capacity indicator; higher means more own-source revenue relative to standard expenditure. |
| **Local finance survey** | 地方財政状況調査 | The national survey of local government accounts. |

## D. Data and methods

| Term | Japanese | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **e-Stat** | e-Stat | The Japanese government's official statistics portal. |
| **IPSS / 社人研** | 国立社会保障・人口問題研究所 | The National Institute of Population and Social Security Research; publishes municipal population projections. |
| **Official land price** | 地価公示 | Annual official land-price assessments at standard sites. |
| **Prefectural land price survey** | 都道府県地価調査 | Annual prefectural land-price assessments, usually 1 July. |
| **Transaction price information** | 不動産取引価格情報 | MLIT's database of reported real-estate transactions. |
| **Building starts statistics** | 建築着工統計 | Statistics on new construction starts. |
| **Building loss statistics** | 建築物滅失統計 | Statistics on demolished/lost buildings; incomplete coverage. |
| **National land numerical information** | 国土数値情報 | MLIT's GIS datasets (boundaries, rail, roads, etc.). |
| **Standard error** | 標準誤差 | A measure of how much an estimate would vary across samples; larger for small samples. |
| **Panel data** | パネルデータ | Data with repeated observations of the same units over time. |
| **Fixed effects** | 固定効果 | A regression method that controls for time-invariant differences between units. |
| **Principal component analysis** | 主成分分析 (PCA) | A method for combining correlated indicators into components; used as a weighting sensitivity check. |
| **Ecological fallacy** | 生態学的誤謬 | Assuming that a relationship observed at the group level holds for every individual. |
| **Winsorisation** | ウィンザライズ | Capping extreme values at a percentile to reduce outlier influence. |
| **Empirical Bayes** | 経験ベイズ | Shrinking noisy small-area estimates toward a broader mean to stabilise them. |
